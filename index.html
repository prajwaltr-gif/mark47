Philosophy & Craft

A Living Sanctuary for Coffee Seekers

Aura & Bean was conceived around a simple revelation: great coffee tastes even more profound when enjoyed in a space that breathes. Surrounded by lush fiddle leaf figs, monstera vines, and warm timber, our café is designed as an urban retreat from the rush of modern life.

Direct Farm Trade

We partner directly with family estates in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, paying 40-50% over fair-trade benchmarks.

In-House Drum Roasting

Roasted in small 12kg batches using our vintage cast-iron roaster to preserve delicate terroir floral and fruit aromas.

Artisanal Viennoiserie

Our pastry chefs begin lamination at 4:00 AM daily, crafting 81 flaky golden layers using pure Normandy butter.

Botanical Lounge

Featuring 200+ indoor botanical plants, high-speed Wi-Fi, sun-drenched reading nooks, and a heated glass patio.
